1. Executive Summary & Problem Addressed

In traditional educational examination systems, centralized question paper storage and premature distribution create single points of failure. Insider leakage, bribed custodians, and interception during transit compromise nationwide exams.

Blockchain Exam Vault eliminates these failure modes using zero-trust cryptographic guarantees:

  • No Single Custodian Can Leak: The paper encryption key is divided into 5 polynomial shares. Any 2 colluding custodians possess strictly 0 bits of information regarding the AES key. At least 3 independent keyholders must cooperate.
  • Multi-Paper Uncertainty: Custodians commit key shares for 3 candidate papers (A, B, and C) before the exam. Neither staff nor colleges know which paper will be selected until the exam deadline.
  • Unbiased Random Selection: Paper choice is deterministically computed on-chain via the Keccak-256 hash of XOR'd secret entropy seeds submitted by the custodians at deadline.
  • Economic Collateral & Automatic Slashing: Each keyholder deposits a 1.0 ETH stake. Failure to submit required seeds or key shares triggers on-chain slashing of 50% (0.50 ETH penalty).
  • Non-Transferable Venue Identity: Question paper download is gated by an ERC-5192 Soulbound Token permanently bound to the authorized college exam centre wallet.

5. Phase 3: Entropy Reveal & Pseudo-Random Paper Selection

At the exam deadline, Custodians 1, 2, and 3 reveal their secret 256-bit entropy seeds. The smart contract verifies each revealed seed against its sealed hash using keccak256(vault, staff, seed) == committedHash.

Figure 5: Administrator Portal (#/admin) showing Phase 3 Unlocked state, Paper B selected, and quorum reached. Paper B Chosen

Mathematical Paper Selection Algorithm

When the seed window expires or all seeds arrive, selectPaper() computes the paper index by XOR-combining all valid revealed seeds:

combinedSeed = seed_1 ^ seed_2 ^ ... ^ seed_k;
selectedPaper = uint8(uint256(keccak256(abi.encodePacked(combinedSeed))) % 3);

In this execution, the algorithm selected Paper B. Only Paper B's pieces can now be accepted.

Unbiasable Randomness Guarantee

Even if 2 custodians are malicious and wait to reveal their seeds last, they cannot manipulate the outcome. The seed challenge window forces seed reveals to conclude before the result is evaluated, preventing selective withholding attacks.
